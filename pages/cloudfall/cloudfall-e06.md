# Cloudfall Episode 6: "Stop Trying to Make 'Gambeson' Happen"

*People Being Other People · Season 2: Cloudfall*

A night aboard the Heavenly Feather turns into a spy story: something tiny and invisible is stuck to Doran's jacket, and the quietest person on the ship knows exactly what it is.

## Episode Summary

Caught red-handed in his office, **Captain Yarhar** sends **Eryn** off with a warning and a guard on her door. Over dinner, **Poppy** serves the crew Italian Dunkers, Eryn starts a rumor by not knowing what they are, and the engineers **DeWalter** and **Makeda** explain that the Rolling Pin can now go ludicrously fast.

**Doran** catches **Rolan Thunder** staring at his shoulder. In a closet full of rope, Eryn finds something tiny and invisible stuck to Doran's jacket. In the cargo hold, Poppy identifies it: a miniature echo bell, a listening device. **Journey** remembers exactly when it was planted.

Doran takes Rolan for a walk on the moonlit deck to get the truth. What comes out is a piece of Doran's own past in Dawn's Edge, and a confession from Rolan, who says something into the bug before he throws it into the clouds.

Meanwhile Eryn's new guard, a kite glider named **Kesher**, loses her to a perfect sneak, and Eryn spends the night in the crow's nest. By morning Yarhar has turned his request into captain's orders.

Then the ship drops through a break in the clouds into **Lower Cumula**, a dark layer full of lightning, where anyone wearing levitation gear starts floating off the deck. Ahead, at last, is the **Isle of Lor**.
