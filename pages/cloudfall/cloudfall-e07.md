# Cloudfall Episode 7: "Sweet Life on Deck"

*People Being Other People · Season 2: Cloudfall*

The party lands on the Isle of Lor, where knowledge can only be bought with stories, and telling theirs pulls everyone back to the day they met.

## Episode Summary

The last morning on the Heavenly Feather is a breakfast rush. **Poppy** sells out to the crew while **Guy** parks his head by the oven to watch the trays, **Journey** gets a cake baked just for her, and **Captain Yarhar** delivers a strange gift from the ship's chef along with a hint that the party's passage wasn't free.

The ship lands on the **Isle of Lor**. Yarhar tells **Eryn** to her face that he doesn't trust her, **Rolan Thunder** warns that something is happening in the clouds, and the tortle prime archivist **Hubert** welcomes them with a birthday cake no one can swallow.

Lor is a library of 119 basalt towers, and its knowledge can't be bought with gold, only with stories. One tower is off limits: the tower of curses, sealed after a great sacrifice. It's exactly where Guy would need to go.

In a dark, incense-filled hut, the archivist **April** collects the party's stories, and they become shared memories. **Doran** recruits a struggling baker and her bodyguard, then catches a starving thief robbing their cart. Poppy and Guy relive the day they met and the night his head first came off.

Then Journey remembers how she met Eryn, and the memory won't let her go. She falls into a cold dark above a distant planet, sees a face she hasn't seen in a very long time, and hears a single word.
