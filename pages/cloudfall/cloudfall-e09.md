# Cloudfall Episode 9: "Recollections and Rave Caves"

*People Being Other People · Season 2: Cloudfall*

Guy finally hears one of his lost memories, and wishes he hadn't, while the party steals a key, meets a sky-sized bird, and stumbles into the Isle of Lor's best-kept secret.

## Episode Summary

The standoff at Tower 13 nearly turns violent until **Doran** casts Calm Emotions. Hiding nearby, **Journey** meets **Tulley**, a giant tortle carrying a whistle that summons **Kree**, a great beast: a bird as big as a sky ship, with cyan and magenta tail feathers, who eats whoever she chooses.

The party regroups and argues. **Poppy** and Journey want to follow the rules, **Eryn** wants to steal, and **Guy** wants his memories back. When the young tortle **Mikey** is caught eavesdropping, the party scares him, bribes him with chocolate, and Eryn lifts his junior archivist's key right off his neck.

At an evening meeting, prime archivist **Hubert** gives Guy one jar of his own past. In it, Guy, a general of **Lodom**, orders a dam that saves the region and drowns the village of **Mako** with some 200 people inside. His other memories end the same way.

Poppy finds Guy hacking at a tree, convinced he has nothing to make up for, and pins a forget-me-not to his collar. Then a mysterious thudding leads them to **Tower 33**, where the young tortles have built a secret underground dance club out of echo bells and dripping cave drums.

Doran wins the dance-off, but Journey's faerie fire reveals something strange in his eyes. Then one of the echo bells picks up the Heavenly Feather's miners in the Bismyte caves, and this actual play D&D podcast episode of Cloudfall ends as the whole island begins to shake.
