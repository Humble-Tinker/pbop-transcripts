# Cloudfall — S1E09: "Recollections and Rave Caves"
**People Being Other People · Season 2 · Cloudfall · Episode 9 · Runtime ~1:19**

---

## Full Summary

The standoff at Tower 13 holds: Shred draws a bo staff, Guy insists his history belongs to him, and Doran casts Calm Emotions; both tortles fail to resist, and a protective charm falls from Hubert's shell. Hubert sends "April" (Eryn) off and sets a meeting for the evening. Journey, hiding in the bushes, meets the huge tortle **Tulley**, who nearly blows an island whistle that calls **Kree**, a great beast: a titanic bird with iridescent cyan-and-magenta tail feathers whose shadow then sweeps the island. Regrouped in a clearing, the party argues: Poppy and Journey want to follow the rules, Eryn wants to steal, Doran wants to lie low, and Guy wants his memories. Mikey, eavesdropping, falls out of hiding; Journey intimidates him, Poppy bribes him with chocolate, and Eryn lifts his **junior archivist's key** from his neck on the walk to the Rolling Pin, where Mikey gorges on chocolate chips and gives Poppy a latching echo bell. The party examines the enchanted stone key, jokes about forging one from Mikey's cake, and hides it in the brush. At the evening meeting, Hubert presents a single jar of Guy's own memory: in the first spring after the upheaval, as Lodom's workers fled to Farewell, Guy ordered a dam that saved the region and drowned the village of **Mako** and some 200 civilians. His other memories end the same way. Eryn confesses to April that she impersonated her. Journey tells Hubert the rules feel harsh, and Hubert explains Lor learned that giving freely brings little back. Poppy finds Guy hacking at a tree; he insists he once wielded true power and has nothing to make up for, and she pins a forget-me-not to his tea towel. The thudding they hear comes from **Tower 33**, where the young tortles have built an underground dance club from echo bells wired to dripping cave drums. Doran's breakdance under Journey's faerie fire reveals his eyes, one a purple spinning ring, the other blue flame. One echo bell catches the Heavenly Feather's miners complaining about dead Bismyte, and as the party heads to warn Hubert, the whole island begins to shake.
