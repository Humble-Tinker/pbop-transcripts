# Cloudfall — S1E02: "Breaking Blight"
**People Being Other People · Season 2 · Cloudfall · Episode 2 · Runtime ~58 min**

---

## Full Summary

Picking up mid-confrontation at Diver's office door, episode 2 is almost entirely a single extended combat encounter. The party is immediately blinded by Tic's accidental flashbang, then wreathed in Diver's fairy fire. Eryn retrieves her personal item — a two-faced wooden carving — from Diver's desk and retreats, leaving the others to manage the fight. The central objective, the large floating Bismyte crystal, is grabbed by Poppy and Pinch. Guy decapitates himself on Diver's fist, loses his head, continues fighting headlessly, and frightens Diver with a Wrathful Smite. Tic destroys the HQ's foundation with a mega-flashbang. Doran and Guy fall 100 feet to the Forge Ring below. Eryn watches from outside as Diver escapes the collapsing building on an unfinished airship, propelling it with raw Bismyte. The party is scattered across the city at episode end — and someone is still falling.

  - Poppy: uses Mage Hand to try to move the Bismyte crystal · learns it's locked · only a magically-inclined person's physical touch can unlock it · **runs across the room and touches it** · feels brief exhilaration · crystal unlocks and moves freely · **grabs it** · eats Pronto Puff · runs for the hallway
  - Diver: Compelled Duel holds · attacks Guy (nat 20) · Doran reacts with **Silvery Barbs** · forces reroll (17) · Guy still hit · 5 bludgeoning damage · **Guy's head knocked clean off** · rolls across the floor toward Eryn
  - Guy: *"Poppy, it happened again."* · Poppy scoops up the head · Guy's body continues fighting

- **Round 4 — Headless Guy frightens Diver**
  - Poppy: holds Guy's head · hands it to his body; Guy reattaches it on backwards · turns it around
  - Journey: Feybuds spot their kin in a terrarium on Diver's shelf
  - Diver: crosses room to terrarium · **punches through it** · grabs the captive Feybud · holds it to the Bismyte crystal · tears it apart · luminous liquid color spills and fades to shadow · Bismyte charges from green to indigo to blue · shadowy puddles on the floor bloom into a **bestial wolf-like specter**
  - Journey: falls to her knees · all Feybuds droop · *"No."* · shoots Diver with bow (22 to hit) · **12 damage** · arrow sticks in his stomach · Feybuds go through his shoulders
  - Guy (opportunity attack): **11 damage** · Wrathful Smite (21 to hit) · **15 damage** · Diver wisdom save fails (6) · **Diver is frightened** · staggers back · visibly sweating for the first time

- **Round 5 — Tic's play**
  - Tic (in hallway): *"Diver... I'm afraid this is farewell."* · hurls a mega-flashbang (multiple capsules combined) through the office into the bay window
  - Bay window shatters · explosion peels up the floorboards · **entire structure begins to lean**
  - Diver: spits Bismyte bullet at Poppy · **12 piercing damage** · then heads for the dock door
  - Opportunity attacks as Diver crosses the room:
    - Guy: misses (10)
    - Journey: hand axe swipe · **4 damage**
    - Doran: rapier · **6 damage**
  - Diver stumbles · visibly bloodied · still moving

- **Round 6 — Collapse**
  - Eryn: outside on a 5-inch service walkway · shimmy to the dock · readied crossbow shot at Diver through the door · misses (14) · waits in darkness
  - Diver: *"I gave you everything. A place to sleep, food to eat, a job, a purpose."* · Eryn (internally): *"You gave me nothing."* · doesn't respond aloud
  - Diver exits onto dock · boards the unfinished airship
  - Tic to Eryn (hallway): *"Aaron, what the fuck are you doing?"* · Eryn: *"You gotta figure it out for yourself."* · Tic: *"Hope I find you again someday."* · Eryn gives him a rude hand gesture
  - Journey: dangling from hallway wreckage by her clothes · 100 feet up · pulls herself back (Athletics 15 + bardic inspiration) · shoots Diver one last time (unnatural 20) · **14 damage** · Feybuds follow the arrow · calls them back · **crouches on the hallway floor crying**
  - Doran: falls from collapsing structure · casts Healing Word on himself mid-fall · eyes closed
  - Guy: falls alongside Doran · removes head to protect it · Form of Dread activates · billowing black smoke · lands on a **tin roof in the Forge Ring** (~100ft fall, reduced by Pronto Puff)
  - Poppy: in the hallway with the Bismyte in her empty cookie basket · Guy and Doran have fallen · sits with Journey · **both quietly crying**

- **Diver escapes**
  - Eryn watches from outside: Diver spits remaining Bismyte · reforms it into a hovering ring around his body
  - The unfinished airship — no spark engine, shouldn't fly — **begins to float**
  - Diver moves with speed and grace surpassing anything seen at the Break Day launch
  - Becomes a dot on the horizon

## Lore & World

### Bismyte — New Properties
- **Color = charge level:** ROYGBIV reversed · red weakest · green = powerful · indigo/blue = extremely powerful
- **Locked state:** floats in midair when charged · must be unlocked by a magic-user's physical touch
- **Consumption:** Diver eats it to power crystalline armor plating and spitting molten Bismyte bullets
- **Biomantic flight:** Diver's ring-form around his body propels him and a full airship at high speed — no spark engine needed

### Fairy Fire
- Diver's signature opener · cold violet flames · does not burn
- Illuminates targets (all attack rolls against them have advantage)
- Persists until Diver takes sufficient damage

### The Wolf Specter
- Born from Diver tearing apart a captive Feybud using Bismyte as a catalyst
- Bestial shadow wolf · lunged at Guy · could not pierce his armor
- **Status: fell from the collapsing structure · lost in darkness**

### Pinch
- Poppy's familiar · a living dough construct
- Can animate, move independently, and attempt physical attacks
- Cannot meaningfully harm a creature like Diver · can be weaponized as a distraction
