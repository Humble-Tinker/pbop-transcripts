# Cloudfall — S1E03: "Falling Apart"
**People Being Other People · Season 2 · Cloudfall · Episode 3 · Runtime ~1:20**

---

## Full Summary

Picking up with Guy and Doran mid-fall and the building collapsing beneath the others, episode 3 is about aftermath, reunion, and diplomacy. Journey grapples Tic long enough to extract a healing vial, then releases him. Eryn exchanges a final loaded goodbye with him. Rolan Thunder, an Air Genasi new arrival, levitates the survivors safely to ground level. Guy and Doran crash through a metal shop roof into a quenching pool, bluff their way past a gnome foreman and the arriving city marshal — a figure who turns out not to be a marshal at all. Eryn improvises as "Tryndle Breath," fictional noble fiancée to Doran, to extract the group from official scrutiny. The party reconvenes over drinks at Clara's Drip Sipper, where the "marshal" reveals himself as Phynom Green — celebrity illusionist, agent of city leader Libera Nimbus. He offers the party passage on the airship *the Tack* to the Isle of Lor for two weeks, away from Blight retaliation. The party reluctantly agrees. Guy reveals his Dullahan nature to his companions and is met with warmth rather than fear. The episode ends with a long rest at the Winged Spire's diplomatic suite, and Phynom announcing a morning shopping trip.
