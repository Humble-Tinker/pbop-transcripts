# Cloudfall — S1E05: "Pinned Down"
**People Being Other People · Season 2 · Cloudfall · Episode 5 · Runtime ~1:16**

---

## Full Summary

The Blight trio of Mercy, Ribbo, and Derringer close in on the Rolling Pin outside Rune's workshop. Phynom keeps everyone in the cart and covers the escape with a crowd of hundreds of illusory Phynoms while Rolan goes out to get the divining stone. Rolan throws the stone into the cart and goes down; Journey runs back to heal him, but Mercy, a red-haired fire caster, drops Journey with three scorching rays. Doran's Healing Word brings her back up and she dives into the cart. Eryn, disguised as one of the illusions, sneak-attacks Mercy from the crowd, then blinds the area with her new ripcord smoke belt and escapes. The cart, now redisguised as "Taco Taco Taco," limps to the Winged Spire at five miles per hour. Journey bites the cracked divining stone to pieces; Eryn reveals the Blight track members through their blood, which Mercy collected while raising the Blight's children, and scrapes Mercy's blood off her dagger into a jar. The party boards the **Heavenly Feather**, a Dawn's Edge ship captained by the reformed pirate **Yarhar**, who already knows Eryn by name, and Rolan joins the voyage. The ship nose-dives off the cliff and levels out over the clouds; Yarhar explains the cloud strata and his three rules, the third being to stay out of the clouds. The party splits up to explore: Poppy helps a bullywug chef in the galley, Guy and Doran get the Rolling Pin's regulator removed by the engine crew, and Eryn maps Yarhar's secret stairwell. The episode ends with Eryn back in the captain's office, about to pick his desk locks, only to find Yarhar sitting there.
