# Cloudfall — S1E06: "Stop Trying to Make 'Gambeson' Happen"
**People Being Other People · Season 2 · Cloudfall · Episode 6 · Runtime ~1:19**

---

## Full Summary

Caught in his office, Captain Yarhar lets Eryn off with a warning (*"let a good thing be a good thing"*) and a guard on her door. At dinner, Poppy serves the crew her Italian Dunkers, Eryn starts a rumor by not knowing what they are, and the engineers DeWalter and Makeda warn Poppy that the Rolling Pin's levitator can now spin a full 360 degrees and that it shouldn't be pushed past 10 mph. Doran's natural-20 insight catches Rolan staring at his shoulder. In a rope closet, Eryn finds a tiny invisible object stuck to Doran's jacket and re-sticks it with old shoe gum; in the cargo hold Poppy identifies it as a miniature echo bell, a listening device, and Journey places the moment it was planted: Libera's too-familiar pat on Doran's shoulder in E04. Doran takes Rolan for a walk on the moonlit deck. After Doran pretends to badmouth Libera, Rolan admits the bug was "mean," confirms Libera is listening in case the party runs into Diver, and says they are not bait. Doran reveals he grew up best friends with Libera's brother in Dawn's Edge during the revolution. Rolan tears the bug off Doran's jacket, apologizes to Libera through it, throws it overboard, and says he'll stay with the ship while the party is on Lor. Meanwhile Eryn's guard, the kite-glider Kesher, loses her to a natural-20 sneak, and Eryn sleeps in the crow's nest. In the morning Yarhar turns his concern about Eryn into captain's orders, and Guy talks him down. The ship drops through a break into Lower Cumula, a dark layer lit by constant lightning, where Rolan and Poppy, in her loafers of levitation, start floating away. The episode ends on the first sight of the Isle of Lor, a cluster of floating basalt-column islands joined by rope bridges.
