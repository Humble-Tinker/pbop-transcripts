# Cloudfall — S1E04: "À La Cart"
**People Being Other People · Season 2 · Cloudfall · Episode 4 · Runtime ~1:25**

---

## Full Summary

Departure morning in Farewell. Before the party can leave, Phynom detours them to meet Libera Cambria Nimbus, the city's leader, in her office at the top of the Winged Spire — where they also briefly meet the city's white-haired co-founder, a man literally named Farewell. Libera thanks them for facing down Diver, gifts them a large blue-violet Bismyte crystal (about half of Diver's whole collection), and buys their silence about the previous night's revelations: the fake creature that became something else, Bismyte gaining power instead of expending it, and an engineless airship. She confirms their destination is the skyland of Lor aboard the Tack, a Dawn's Edge guild ship, and admits she can no longer offer protection once they leave the city today. Phynom and Rolan then take the party — disguised by Phynom's illusions as rotating groups (schoolchildren, an adventuring party, dancers) — to the Lake Orthyr marketplace to provision. At Jayson Chalzone's Discount Magic Items, a secondhand shop, Guy claims the end-heavy Blade of Lodom (which he wields with uncanny natural skill), Journey trades her handmade Firbolg bow for a glowing radiant bow, Doran buys a lock-stock dagger and the malfunctioning "Owly" statuette, Poppy gets loafers of levitation and an enlarging belt, and Eryn picks up a smoke-belt escape tool. Poppy uses a suggestibility toffee to haggle the sword down. In the market the party also runs into two of Journey's Firbolg kin — now renamed Frydough and Sellum — struggling to understand commerce, and Poppy leaves them her mother's fry-bread recipe and a lead on the food-famous skyland of Saffron. The group reunites the Bismyte-powered Rolling Pin cart (redisguised by Phynom as "the Tough Cookie"), and Doran quietly drops a chunk of the spare Bismyte at Rune's derelict workshop as a gift. The episode ends on a cliffhanger: Blight members Ribbo, Derringer, and Mercy track the cart to Rune's area with a divining stone, forcing the party to flee for the docks.

## Key Events

### Part 1 — The Detour to Libera
*(00:00 – 00:12)*

- **Departure morning**
  - Bright morning at Clara's · the party prepping to leave Farewell
  - Poppy doles out pre-baked cookies to anyone "afraid of falling" — she baked them in a coffee maker that morning
  - Poppy hands Guy a Pronto Puff and Doran a toffee temptation: *"Do not eat this one right away."*
  - Phynom announces a detour: *"There's somebody I want you to meet. It's Libera."*

- **Rolan reassures Eryn**
  - Rolan pulls Eryn aside: he's watched the Blight — and Eryn — for a long time · *"We don't wanna use you for anything. We want you to be who you are."*
  - Eryn, guarded: *"What I can do, it has only gotten me into bad places."*
  - Offers an official pardon if it comes to that
  - Eryn decides to stay with the group for now

- **The climb**
  - Hundreds of stairs up the Winged Spire · Libera famously uses lifts and flying inventions instead
  - Giant canvas "wings" on the tower's side function as a colossal weather vane for the city's airship traffic

### Part 2 — Libera's Office
*(00:12 – 00:29)*

- **Meeting Farewell**
  - Libera is mid-meeting with an old white-haired man she introduces as **Farewell** — the city's co-founder, who shares its name
  - A running bit: every time someone says "Farewell" in parting, he turns and nods
  - Farewell exits on *"Time is of the essence."*

- **The introductions**
  - Libera is relaxed, down-to-earth · workers' clothes over red-and-violet official garb · long black braid
  - Poppy introduces herself and the Rolling Pin · Doran reveals he's from **Dawn's Edge** (she pegs him instantly)
  - Journey offers her name; Libera guesses Firbolg correctly · Journey dubs her "leads-the-city-from-above" — Libera loves it
  - Libera already knows Eryn by name, to Eryn's visible discomfort

- **The Bismyte gift**
  - Libera flips a canvas to reveal a large Bismyte crystal — blue-violet, high quality, about half of Diver's whole collection
  - Offers it as thanks · *"I'm prepared to give this to you for what you've done so far."*
  - Guy confesses they were after the Bismyte all along · Libera: *"You chose a good target."*

- **The price: silence**
  - The condition — ask no questions about last night: the fake creature that became something else, Bismyte gaining power, an engineless airship
  - Libera: *"I'll be specific this one time and never again."*
  - She's handling Diver herself, through Phynom and Rolan · doesn't want the party's help hunting him

- **Libera and the clouds**
  - Libera confesses her obsession with the world beyond the clouds · she's been taking trips out there since childhood
  - *"It bewilders me that common folk know nothing about the clouds."* · *"I can hardly stand to be here."*
  - Confirms destination: the skyland of **Lor**, aboard the **Tack** (Dawn's Edge guild) · party will be honored guests, effectively the only passengers
  - Warns: after today, once they leave Farewell, she can't protect them

### Part 3 — Lake Orthyr & the Magic Shop
*(00:29 – 01:01)*

- **Disguised for the market**
  - Crossing the threshold outside, Phynom illusion-disguises the party as schoolchildren · Rolan plays teacher: *"This way, class."*
  - The disguise rotates through the episode: schoolkids → classic adventuring party → dancers in leotards
  - Destination: **Lake Orthyr**, Farewell's island marketplace ringing a lake

- **Jayson Chalzone's Discount Magic Items**
  - A humble, well-kept secondhand shop the party mistakes for a pizza place
  - Proprietor: **Jayson Chalzone**, a massive crimson tiefling with ceiling-scraping horns
  - Guy eyes a sword in the window — *"It seems familiar to me somehow."*

- **The Blade of Lodom**
  - Doran can barely lift it — freakishly end-heavy, feels 3× its weight
  - Guy takes it and it swings into place naturally, *"like it was meant to be there"* · Chalzone: *"I've never seen such a good fit"*
  - Chalzone smells the blade — *"blood"* · asking price 250 gold
  - **Poppy's haggle**: feeds Chalzone a suggestibility toffee (honeycomb bite) · he fails the save · she talks the price down to ~60 gold equivalent (50 gold + 100 silver), Guy chipping in silver

- **Journey's bow trade**
  - Two bows: one glows and burns radiant on a held draw; the other grants a second shot on a miss
  - Journey trades in her handmade Firbolg bow · Doran coaches her to play up "artisanal, made by Firbolgs"
  - Persuasion at disadvantage (she doesn't look Firbolg while disguised) · rolls a 7 · Doran salvages it
  - Lands the glowing bow · net 285 gold with the trade-in

- **More purchases**
  - Poppy: **loafers of levitation** (85g, Phynom pays) · later an enlarging belt (haggled 400→25g)
  - Doran: a **lock-stock dagger** (sticks in midair — nat 20 dexterity save for a backflip dismount) · Phynom covers it under 550g · also **"Owly,"** a chipped, malfunctioning owl statuette (15g)
  - Eryn: a repeatable smoke-and-flash escape belt · tries and rejects uncomfortable sneaking slippers
  - Phynom caps it at "one thing each — don't tell Libera"

### Part 4 — The Market, Journey's Kin & the Getaway
*(01:01 – 01:25)*

- **Blight sighting**
  - Eryn spots Derringer (weasel-ish, southern accent) and a woman, **Mercy** — real name **Brunhilda Flann**, who insists on being called Mercy
  - Eryn uses "thieves' cant" doublespeak (*"the clouds are looking a little green"*) to warn Phynom · fails to teach it to Rolan

- **Journey's Firbolg kin**
  - Journey runs into two old friends from her tribe, now in Farewell trying to make money
  - They've renamed themselves by function: **Frydough** (was Nureshall — *"I no longer nourish all"*) and **Sellum** (was Goodlook — *"I sell them now"*)
  - They don't understand charging money for bread · Journey and Poppy try to explain commerce
  - Poppy writes down her mother's fry-bread recipe · they gift the party gold instead of taking it, and it's gently pushed back
  - Frydough recommends the food-famous skyland of **Saffron** — instantly Poppy's new life goal

- **The Tough Cookie**
  - The Rolling Pin cart runs on a Bismyte levitator — a precursor to the spark engine · bigger on the inside, a full bakery with bunks
  - Guy loads the gifted Bismyte crystal into the furnace · blue nuclear-startup glow · the shop levitates
  - Phynom redisguises the cart itself as **"the Tough Cookie"**: red-and-black, witch hat, chains, a giant spiked iron cookie on the front · Pinch celebrates in his olive-oil bowl

- **The Bismyte gift to Rune**
  - Doran breaks off a chunk of spare Bismyte (~1 pound, ~150g value) to give to Rune
  - Leaves it at the door of Rune's derelict salvage workshop · a metallic voice answers · Doran keeps it anonymous

- **Cliffhanger — the divining stone**
  - Guy spots (Perception 17) Ribbo, Derringer, and Mercy using a **divining stone** — a pendulum that bobs toward its target — homing in on the cart
  - Eryn confirms: it's a Blight tool for tracking people who wronged them, and it's tracking *her*
  - Mercy points: *"They're in there."*
  - Journey turns invisible (Hidden Step) to retrieve Doran · Guy: *"Poppy, start the cart."* · they run for the docks
