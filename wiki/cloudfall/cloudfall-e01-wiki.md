# Cloudfall — S1E01: "This Is Farewell"
**People Being Other People · Season 2 · Cloudfall · Episode 1 · Runtime ~1:34**

---

## Full Summary

Break Day — the 15th anniversary of Farewell's founding — is underway. The city erupts in celebration as the Guilds launch their first Bismyte-powered airships into the clouds. Five strangers converge on the quietest place in Farewell: Clara's Bar, a gnome's nerve-wracking grand opening. Through Doran's arrangement, they form an uneasy alliance to recover Bismyte from Diver, the leader of the Blight criminal organization, who is also holding a bounty on former member Eryn. A chance encounter with Rune's warforged messenger Sir Beau produces echo bells for the two-team operation. The team splits — Doran, Guy, and Eryn through the Blight's back door posing as fake bounty hunters; Poppy and Journey through the front as cookie salespeople. Eryn kills Lob and shapeshifts into his form; Journey kills Barb and Thompson; the teams reunite inside. The episode ends mid-confrontation as the party reaches Diver's office door.

## Lore & World

### Bismyte
- Heavy silvery metal · depleted form is common and considered hazardous
- Charged Bismyte powers the spark engine · rare and valuable
- The specific target of this mission

### Spark Engine
- New invention enabling Bismyte-powered airship flight
- Every guild racing to build a vessel · today is the first mass public launch

### The Guilds
- **Lodom** · **Cilivry** · others — each debuting a flagship on Break Day

### Echo Bells
- Paired ammonite fossils tuned as communicators · made by Rune
- Must be held to the ear; voice transmits to the paired shell
- Perform poorly under pressure (see: Guy)

### Pink Meridian Blossoms
- Grow only in the exact center of the Feywild · dawn to dusk
- Most dangerous Feywild territory · ~500gp per cup of wine

### Pronto Puffs & Pop Offs
- Magical baked goods · made by Poppy
- **Pronto Puff:** prevents fall damage for 10 minutes
- **Pop Off:** on attack roll 15+, chain-roll until lower; crit adds 1d12 force + push 10ft

### Feybuds
- Tiny fey creatures living in Journey's hair · quarter-sized neon sparkling orbs
- Semi-sentient · assist attacks · can pierce ethereally
