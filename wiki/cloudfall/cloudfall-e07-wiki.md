# Cloudfall — S1E07: "Sweet Life on Deck"
**People Being Other People · Season 2 · Cloudfall · Episode 7 · Runtime ~1:30**

---

## Full Summary

The last morning aboard the Heavenly Feather turns into a breakfast rush at the Rolling Pin: Guy parks his detached head by the oven window to watch the trays while his body works, Poppy sells about 50 gold of cookies to the crew, gives Journey a protective fiber field cake and Rolan a saved maple cookie, and receives a spell-catching pewter ladle from Chef Bowie by way of Captain Yarhar, who reveals the party's passage wasn't free; someone paid. The ship lands on a moss field on the Isle of Lor. Yarhar tells Eryn plainly that he doesn't trust her and has two burly sailors walk her off; Rolan warns the party there is a lot happening in the clouds right now and stays aboard. The tortle prime archivist **Hubert** and his nephew **Mikey** greet them with an inedible cauliflower birthday cake. Lor holds 119 natural basalt towers, each a library on one subject. Knowledge can't be bought with gold, only with knowledge: the archivists record spoken stories in smoke-filled jars that turn white for truth, black for known lies, and gray for fiction, and pay for them in "chapters." Tower 12 holds fey magic; Tower 13, the curses tower, is sealed after a "great sacrifice," and entering it likely means death. Five Heavenly Feather crew stay behind to mine the island's Bismyte, which Lor's people have always taken for granted. The archivist **April** gathers the party in a dark, incense-filled hut and, under drift weed, their stories play out as shared flashbacks: Doran recruiting Poppy and Guy, and catching Eryn robbing the Rolling Pin; Poppy and Guy's first meeting and the brigands who knocked Guy's head off; and Eryn luring Journey away from the Blight's door. Journey's memory doesn't end. She falls into a cold void above a distant blue-green planet, sees a face she hasn't seen in a long time, and hears one word: *"Seek."*

## Key Events

### Part 1 — Breakfast Rush
*(00:00 – 00:14)*

- **The line at the Rolling Pin**
  - Crowds of crew around the cart · Poppy: single file · 10 cookies for 1 gold
  - Guy splits himself: his head on the counter watching the oven window, his body moving trays · rule: the body works as long as the head can see it
  - Journey watches the crowd · her first look at a community outside her own, with budging and all

- **Gifts**
  - Rolan hovers shyly near Journey · he really wants a cookie
  - Poppy gives Journey a **fiber field cake** made especially for her (see Lore)
  - Rolan gets a saved chewy maple cookie · wraps it in a napkin to save it

- **Yarhar's turnover**
  - Yarhar avoids Eryn on deck · Eryn: *"I'm here, you big lug."*
  - Last in line, Yarhar takes an apple turnover and pays anyway · *"Can't go wrong with fruit at sea."* · Doran: *"He's a captain, not a fruitologist."*
  - *"You're not paying, but it wasn't free."*
  - Gift from **Chef Bowie**: a pewter ladle that catches small spells and flings them back · Bowie used it on drunk magic users when working in bars
  - Poppy's take: about 50 gold · GM ruling: 1 gold = 10 cookies

### Part 2 — Landing on Lor
*(00:14 – 00:27)*

- **Touchdown**
  - The ship descends onto a nearly flat field of moss lit by a shaft of sun · 25-foot oars brace it upright on land
  - The island is miles wide · much of it hidden in cloud like an iceberg

- **Goodbyes**
  - Yarhar to Eryn: he wants to start over in two weeks · *"I don't know you. I don't trust you. I'm sorry."* · Eryn: *"I don't trust you either."*
  - Guy's vigorous handshake · Doran: *"I'll never forget your ship, the heavenly father."*
  - Two burly sailors walk Eryn off the ship
  - Rolan eats a bite of the cookie · *"Incredible."*
  - Rolan: *"Be safe out there... there's a lot happening in the clouds right now."* · stays aboard: *"There's other things I need to see out here."* · be gentle, the people here aren't used to outsiders
  - The singing elf starts a farewell song (*"From Farewell to Farewell"*)

- **The Rolling Pin's grand exit**
  - Poppy starts the levitator · the oven goes cold · she accidentally floors it down the gangplank past the waiting tortle
  - Guy runs it down and drags it back · *"Oh, it can go 10, baby."*

- **Hubert**
  - **Hubert, prime archivist** · old tortle hung with charms and ropes, lamp and staff
  - Yarhar hands over five crew who are staying to mine
  - Hubert insists on proper names · *"I understand you are a guy."* · to Doran: *"Humor. I like it."* · to Eryn: *"Are you a guy?"* · *"Maybe."*
  - Inspects Journey's Feybuds · *"A parasite, perhaps lice"* · Journey: symbiotic, they're fey · *"You must seek tower 12."*

### Part 3 — The Archivist's Tour
*(00:27 – 00:56)*

- **The birthday cake**
  - Nephew **Mikey** holds out a birthday cake · *"It's all of our birthday today because you are here."*
  - Guy: *"I've never celebrated a birthday before."* · Poppy is mortified she never baked him one

- **The towers**
  - 119 natural hexagonal basalt towers, hollowed into libraries, one subject each
  - Magic (1–3), wind and water, plants and food (Tower 96), fire and stone, inventions, stories, religion, creatures, other worlds · *"Dewey Decimal but super fucked up"*
  - All the same height, just shifted up or down · Poppy: *"That's horrifying."*
  - Mikey must not be spoken to or taught · he'll earn the right to speak

- **Paying in knowledge**
  - Gold buys lodging, food, and comfort · knowledge must be paid for in knowledge
  - Guy considers paying with the chicken and the egg

- **The warming house**
  - Cobbled hexagonal house in a misty juniper forest · fire, armchairs, four private rooms · free for honored guests
  - The five crew paid in advance · Mikey takes them to the mining equipment
  - The cake: dense, fibrous, a cauliflower base · Poppy fails Con (5) and hides it in her apron (Sleight of Hand 17) · *"I'm really good at not eating things."* · Guy eats it · Doran fails (12) and spits it into a folded plate
  - Hubert: *"That's Mikey. He's an idiot."*

- **What they seek**
  - Doran: secret societies and real crime · Poppy: recipes, even from Saffron · Journey: Bismyte, and the mines · Eryn: changelings, likely only fairy tales
  - Guy asks about his curse · records exist, but **Tower 13**, the curses tower, low on the east end, was sealed after *"a great sacrifice"* · unholy · no penalty to enter, *"though death will likely follow"*
  - Guy settles for records of "cool wars"
  - Bismyte keeps the island afloat · the tortles have always taken it for granted

- **Story jars and chapters**
  - Hubert opens a wooden jar · smoke and a distant conversation · a recorded memory
  - Stories earn **chapters**, which buy knowledge
  - Smoke color: white for truth, black for a falsehood the teller knows, gray for fiction · it can't tell you anything you don't know
  - The archivist **April** will take their stories

- **Doran on thin ice**
  - Under a green blanket: crumbling red-pink depleted Bismyte
  - Doran: Diver is not at the top · there's a larger network
  - Poppy has a bone to pick: this was supposed to be a quick get-rich scheme
  - Eryn: how does Doran know Diver? · *"Yes, I have met Diver before."* · the story will wait

### Part 4 — The Story Circle
*(00:56 – 01:10)*

- **April's hut**
  - April, older than Mikey, younger than Hubert · walks agonizingly slowly
  - A dark, warm, curtained hut deep in the island · pillows in a circle · a jar held over a candle
  - A pinch of **drift weed** burned as incense · *"It just makes you a little chatty"*
  - Doran goes first · white smoke

- **Doran's story: the recruiting**
  - Doran came to Farewell to find Diver · scouted the Blight, their headquarters, their patterns
  - Flashback: buying one of each cookie at the Rolling Pin · business is slow, the cart is out of fuel and Guy pulls it
  - Doran pitches a job: take fuel from those who take from shops like this

- **Doran's story: Eryn**
  - Eryn, starving, sneaks through the bakery behind them · grabs donut holes, skips the till · historical Stealth 13
  - Doran's Perception 15 catches a donut hole hitting the cobbles
  - Rooftop chase · Doran is already on the roof, offering a hand
  - The offer: hit Diver, take enough to leave the city and start over · *"He took a lot from me. I want this to hurt."*
  - Eryn: *"Alright. I'm in."* · Doran gives her 10 gold · meet at the Drip Sipper in a week

### Part 5 — Poppy & Guy
*(01:10 – 01:19)*

- **How they met**
  - Poppy dropped out of the Tower of Magic in Teledry to start a mobile bakery
  - She caught Guy stealing cooling bread · made him work it off · he hadn't eaten in days

- **The brigands**
  - Two brigands stop the cart · the small one does the talking, the big one ("Mancy") does the threatening
  - Guy: *"Haven't any of you ever visited a bakery before? That is not how it works."*
  - A thrown gold coin burns a welt into Guy's face
  - A billy club knocks Guy's head off · his headless body taps the big man on the shoulder
  - Green-flamed greatsword · the big brigand dies · the small one flees screaming *"Monster"* · Poppy throws a cookie at his head

- **The tea towel**
  - Poppy scoops up the head in a tea towel · halfling bravery: *"You don't scare me."*
  - *"I think you've got a good head on your shoulders, Guy."* · ties the towel around his neck in a bow
  - *"I don't make a habit of judging people before I know them, and I already know you."*

- **Guy's account**
  - Guy is a **Dullahan**, between life and death · knows almost nothing of his life before he died
  - People after were unkind · that's why he stays with Poppy

### Part 6 — Journey's Vision
*(01:19 – 01:30)*

- **Journey and Eryn**
  - Journey learned someone in Farewell had caged a Feybud, not one of hers · went to the Blight's door
  - Eryn, staking out the HQ for Doran, watches a Firbolg make a scene
  - Journey knocks: you shouldn't keep a living creature in a box · Lob slams the door
  - A small child (Eryn) asks for help finding a lost toy and leads Journey into a dark alley
  - Eryn reveals herself · Journey's Feybuds turn the alley into a brief rave · Eryn smiles
  - A break-in is coming · meet here tomorrow · *"You can call me Eryn."* · *"You can call me Journey."*

- **Seek**
  - The memory won't release Journey · she goes catatonic in the hut
  - April won't let Doran break it · *"Let her wake naturally."*
  - Journey walks off into open air · treading cold water · far below, a blue-green planet thousands of miles away
  - A face she hasn't seen in so long · *"Hello."* · Journey: *"What would you have me do?"* · *"Seek."*
  - She wakes shaking and drops the water cup · April: *"That's going to be enough for today."*
