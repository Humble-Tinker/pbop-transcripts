# Cloudfall — Locations

*Running registry. Every line cites its episode.*

## Farewell (Arc 1)
- Revolutionary cliff-edge city on the eastern frontier · clouds east, three crumbling kingdoms west (E01)
- **Upper Ledges** — high-society district, settled from Dawn's Edge (E01)
- **Silver Street** — slum strip dusty with depleted Bismyte (E01)
- **Blight HQ** — laundromat front · destroyed (E01–E02)
- **Forge Ring** — industrial metal-shop district (E02–E03)
- **The Drip Sipper** — Clara's bar (E01, E03)
- **Winged Spire** — city tower · diplomatic suite · Libera's office at the top · canvas-wing weather vane (E03–E04)
- **Lake Orthyr** — island marketplace ringed by one street (E04)
- **Rune's Workshop** — derelict salvage storefront near the Forge Ring (E04)

## Elsewhere
- **Dawn's Edge** — western city · home of the Tack guild, builders of the Heavenly Feather (E01, E05)
- **Lodom**, **Cilivry** — guilds / homelands with Break Day flagships (E01)
- **Lodom** — lost its best people to Farewell ("the traitor's citadel") after the upheaval · flood plains (E09)
- **Mako** — river village near Lodom · drowned by Guy's dam (E09 · memory)
- **Lamplight Landing** — where Yarhar nearly died (E05 · mentioned)
- **Vindorn** — great mountain where the worst of the world's curses were stemmed, long ago (E08 · mentioned · spelling unconfirmed)

## Skylands & the clouds
- **Isle of Lor** — destination · in Lower Cumula · cluster of floating basalt-column islands joined by rope bridges, lower half in permanent cloud (E03–E06)
  - Miles wide · moss landing field · stony canyons · misty juniper forest (E07)
  - 119 basalt towers, each a library on one subject, all the same height but shifted up or down (E07)
  - **Tower 12** — fey magic · **Tower 13** — curses, sealed, low on the east end · **Tower 96** — plants and food (E07)
  - **Warming house** — guest house, four rooms · **April's hut** — the story circle (E07)
  - **Tower 13** — east end in a cliff's shadow · ~10 ft above ground, nearly all underground · echo bell alarm on the door · chalk door outline on the back (E08)
  - **Tower 25** — war, torture, crime, atrocities · Guy's records in advanced storage (E08)
  - **Tower 33** — unlocked · seven floors of jars, purpose unknown · basement dance club, 100+ latched echo bells (E09)
  - **Caves** under the island, where cloud water drips onto echo-bell drums · **Bismyte mines**, much of it dead (E09)
  - **West slope** — tall-grass hill near the edge · **bonsai garden** in the low juniper grove · **dining hut** of stone with a mossy roof (E08)
- **Saffron** — food-famous skyland · Poppy's goal (E04)
- **High Shire** — halfling-only skyland (E05)
- **Cloud strata** — regular atmosphere · Upper Cumula · Lower Cumula · Null Substrata (E05)
- **Lower Cumula** — dark as night, violent wind, constant lightning (E06)
- **Tildry** — Tower of Magic Poppy dropped out of (E06–E07)

## Vessels

- **The Rolling Pin** — Poppy's levitating bakery cart · disguised as the Tough Cookie, then Taco Taco Taco · regulator removed (E04–E05)
