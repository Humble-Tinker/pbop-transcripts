## Bismyte
- Chargeable metal · color = charge, red weakest to violet strongest (E02)
- Floats when charged · unlocked by a magic-user's touch · Diver eats and spits it (E02)
- ~1 pound ≈ 150 gold (E04) · measured in "magic pounds" (E05)
- Heavenly Feather carries ~100 magic pounds, ~15,000 gold, paid by Libera (E05)

- **Fairy fire** — Diver's opener (E02)

- **Null Substrata** — magic-cancelling mist; ships that touch it fall (E05)

- **Yarhar's three rules** — don't touch down where you can't take off · don't fly at night · stay out of the clouds (E05)
